# Bank Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project analyzes retail bank customer churn to identify key drivers of customer defection and financial loss. The interactive Power BI dashboard provides insights into demographic trends, account behavior, and product usage to assist stakeholders in developing targeted retention strategies.

---

## 📊 Key Business Metrics
* **Total Customers:** 10,000
* **Overall Churn Rate:** 20.37%
* **Total Capital Lost:** ₹185.59M
* **Average Credit Score:** 650.54

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Findings
1. **Product Risk:** Customers holding **3 or 4 products** show an alarming churn rate between **83% and 100%**, pointing to possible product mis-selling or service dissatisfaction.
2. **Age & Demographics:** Churn rates spike significantly in older age groups (**60+ years**), whereas younger cohorts maintain higher retention.
3. **Geographic Distribution:** Germany exhibits higher customer loss rates relative to total account balances compared to France and Spain.
4. **Behavioral Activity:** Inactive accounts churn at twice the rate of active members, particularly among customers holding credit cards.
